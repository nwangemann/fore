# Review and explorations, 2026-11-25

A first pass over v0.1 to decide what to fix and what to explore next. Status: the suggested next batch (fixes 1–9, explorations 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10) was built the same day; the rest is in the [backlog](../backlog/README.md).

Evidence comes from the live dashboard for Portland, OR and St Andrews on 2026-11-25. Effort is S (under an hour), M (a session) or L (several sessions).

## Verdict

The bones are right: one saved place, keyless sources, fast to load, and a theme with real personality. The main weakness is that **the golf score is absolute**. In a normal November every day is over par (Portland's week was +2, +3, +3, E, +2, +3, +1 = **+14**), so the headline gimmick says "bad" all winter and tells you nothing. Second, the dashboard shows **values but not timing**: it says "Rain 63%" when the useful thing is "rain from 4 am to 9 am".

## What works: keep

- One home place, saved, with search and "Use my location". Matches the "almost always the same place" brief.
- Open-Meteo plus NWS: keyless, CORS-friendly, global, and richer than v0.1 uses.
- The look: mowing stripes, scorecard paper, Shrikhand, red "FORE!" alert banners. The alert pun is the best joke in the app because it's also literally correct.
- Circle and box score marks on the scorecard: instantly readable to anyone who has seen a scorecard.
- The layout holds up at 375 px and on desktop.

## What doesn't make sense yet

### 1. Par should be the seasonal normal, not a fixed ideal (M) — recommended

`scoreDay` treats 18–26 °C as ideal everywhere and always, so winter is a permanent double bogey and the weekly total is noise.

- **Fix:** par is what's normal for this place and date. Fetch the last 10 years of the same ±7-day window from the Open-Meteo archive API (checked: keyless, works), then score each day against that: warmer and drier than normal is under par. Cache the normals per place; they barely change.
- Keep an absolute guard so a "warm for January" day that's still 2 °C doesn't come out as an eagle, e.g. cap at par below some floor.
- **Drop the week total.** Replace it with **"Best day to play: Sat"**, which is something you can act on.

### 2. The golf metaphors collide (S)

- "The front nine · next 18 hours" contradicts itself. Rename it **"The round"**, or split it into two visible halves: _Front nine_ and _Back nine_.
- Hours carry hole numbers 1–18, and the scorecard also calls days "Hole". Drop the numbers on hours; keep "Hole" for days.

### 3. The yardage book mixes "now" with "today" (S)

It's labelled _conditions now_ but holds _Rain today_ (a forecast total for the whole day) and _Tee times_ (sunrise to sunset).

- Move today's rain total into the hero next to High and Low.
- Give sun times their own small strip: _First tee 7:23 am · Last light 4:31 pm · 9 h 8 m_, plus golden hour (see exploration 6).

### 4. Rain needs timing, not just a percentage (M) — recommended

The hero says _Rain 63%_ and _Caddie says: Rain likely today_. The hourly data already knows it's 87–100% from 4 am to 9 am.

- Add a **one-line plain-language outlook** at the top of the hero, built in core from hourly data, e.g. _"Dry this evening. Rain 4–9 am, heaviest around 6. Clearing by noon."_
- Add a **next-two-hours nowcast** from Open-Meteo `minutely_15` (checked: works keyless), e.g. _"Drizzle stops in about 30 min."_

### 5. No context against yesterday (S)

"50°" means more next to "6° warmer than yesterday". `past_days=1` on the same forecast call gives it for free.

### 6. Pressure is in hPa even in imperial, and it has no trend (S)

US readers expect inHg. The trend (rising, steady, falling over 3 h) says more than the number. Hourly pressure gives the trend.

### 7. The pollen section is dead weight outside Europe (S)

For a US home place it permanently says "not tracked". In European winters it shows six rows of "None".

- Outside Europe, hide the section entirely (or leave one muted line) until a US source is chosen. See exploration 4.
- When every species is "None", collapse to one line: _"The rough is quiet."_

### 8. Every open starts from blank (S) — recommended

Nothing is cached, so each visit shows "Teeing up the forecast…", and offline shows an error.

- Persist the last good payload per place. Paint it at once with a "from 12 min ago" note, then refresh. For a dashboard opened many times a day this is the biggest perceived-speed win.

### 9. Smaller correctness items (S each)

- **Follow me** loads the saved place, then the device place: two fetches, a recents entry, and a Nominatim call on every open. Skip the switch when the fix is within about 2 km of the saved place.
- **Settings hydration:** on web, localStorage hydrates before fonts finish, so there's no flash of St Andrews (verified: a reload fetched only Portland). On native, AsyncStorage is async, so the dashboard could fetch the default place first. Wait for hydration before loading.
- **Alerts fail silently.** If NWS is down, "no alerts" and "couldn't check" look the same. Add a small muted note when the check fails.
- **Air is current-only.** Open-Meteo has hourly `us_aqi`, so the card could say _"Smoke rising this afternoon, AQI 120 by 4 pm"_.
- **Contrast:** "Card signed 7:57 PM" is 12 px sand on green at 85% opacity. Raise it to full opacity.
- **Emoji icons** look different on every OS and can't be coloured to match. Fine for now; a small custom SVG set would lift the design a lot (L).

## Feature explorations

Grouped by what they'd give you. ★ marks the recommended picks for a daily-glance dashboard.

### Glance and decide

1. ★ **Today's verdict line:** one sentence at the top: _"Grab a jacket and an umbrella before 9."_ Built from the rules in core; no new data.
2. ★ **Best tee time today:** the best two-hour window from hourly data (dry, light wind, warmest). Useful for a walk, run or round, not just golf.
3. **What to wear:** layers, umbrella and sunscreen as three small icons, driven by "plays like" temperature, rain and UV.
4. **US pollen:** needs a keyed source (Google Pollen, Ambee, Tomorrow.io) behind a tiny proxy such as a Cloudflare Worker. The first piece of infrastructure, so worth deciding deliberately.

### Context

5. ★ **Against normal:** comes with fix 1: _"7° above normal for late November."_
6. **Sky:** golden hour, moon phase and moonrise, all computable offline (`astronomy-engine` covers them).
7. **This day last year:** from the archive API. A small, fun line.

### Maps and alerts

8. **Radar:** RainViewer tiles on a small map. Needs a map library; the heaviest UI addition.
9. **Notifications:** "Rain in 20 min" or "New NWS warning". On web this needs push plus a server; on iOS it needs the native build. Real value, real cost (L).

### Platform

10. ★ **Install to home screen:** a web manifest plus icons, hosted as a static site (`npx expo export -p web`). Makes it feel like an app on your phone with no App Store (S–M).
11. **Hosting:** any static host. Decide whether it's public or behind a password.
12. **iOS widget:** the most "glanceable" format, but it needs the native build plus a widget extension (L). Revisit after living with the PWA.

### Theme and fun

13. ★ **Course changes with the weather:** night turns the stripes to a dark-green night course, snow turns them frosted, a storm adds a red flag. Uses `is_day` and the condition mood that already exist.
14. **Caddie quips with variety:** several quips per condition, rotated daily, so the jokes don't go stale.
15. **"Mulligan":** a button that re-rolls the quip. Pointless and charming.
16. **Actual golf mode:** wind arrow relative to a hole direction, "plays like" yardage adjustment. Only if you actually golf.
